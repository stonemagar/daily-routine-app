How progress is calculated

Completion rate = completed due routines ÷ all due routines. Upcoming routines and skipped routines are excluded. An early completion counts immediately.

A streak counts scheduled days with every non-skipped routine completed. Rest days and fully skipped days are neutral. Today has until midnight to extend a streak. Routine streaks use the same rule for that routine. Streaks follow category and routine filters, across all tracked dates.

Deleting a routine retains its history and stops scheduling it from tomorrow. Schedule edits apply today onward. Old completions without a known schedule contribute to totals, but not rates or streaks. Corrections to past dates have no completion time.
